About the Ma’had

Ma’had Tahfidzhul Qur’an Riyadhus Shalihin was established in 2021 in Ganten Village, Kerjo District, Karanganyar, Central Java. The Ma’had was founded by asatidz and community leaders to nurture a generation of Qur’an memorizers who possess strong character, discipline, and a spirit of da’wah.

Located in the heart of the village, the Ma’had integrates Islamic boarding education with active community engagement, making it a center for Qur’anic learning and religious development for both students and the surrounding community.

Vision & Mission

Vision

Educating students who are huffadz, possess pious character, carry the soul of da’wah, and contribute to the development of a righteous society.

Mission

  • Providing Qur’anic education based on tahsin and mutqin
  • Instilling Ahlussunnah wal Jama’ah values
  • Integrating Qur’anic education with formal learning
  • Developing responsibility and discipline in students
  • Serving the community through da’wah and Qur’anic programs

Education Programs

Ma’had Aly (Post-High School)

An intensive Qur’an memorization and Islamic education program focusing on strengthening da’wah skills and preparing students to serve the community.

Madrasah Program (Junior High Level)

A three-year boarding-based education program focusing on Qur’an memorization, character development, and foundational Islamic knowledge, preparing students for higher education.

Community Outreach

Qur’anic education for children (TPQ), halaqah for mothers, public Islamic studies, mosque services, and community da’wah activities.
